# Foodie 🍽️

> AI-powered meal planner that suggests recipes, generates ingredients, and builds a smart shopping list — so you eat well, plan less, and never buy more than you need.

## What it does

Foodie helps you plan your meals for the week and takes care of everything that comes after:

- **AI meal suggestions** — type a craving or ask the AI to plan your whole week in plain English
- **Instant ingredient generation** — hit Generate Ingredients and Foodie auto-fills everything for the meal
- **Smart shopping list** — combines and deduplicates ingredients across all your meals, grouped by category
- **Pantry tracker** — mark what you already have so it's flagged on your shopping list
- **Meal assistant** — ask questions about a specific meal ("make this dairy free", "substitute for eggs")
- **Duplicate meals** — copy any meal to other days with one click
- **Custom date ranges** — plan by week or pick any date range

## Why I built it

I built Foodie to solve a personal problem — I'd spend too long deciding what to eat, then overbuy at the grocery store and waste food. I wanted a tool that combined meal planning and shopping list generation in one place, with AI doing the heavy lifting.

It also gave me hands-on experience taking a product from 0 to 1: defining the spec, making UX decisions, iterating based on real usage, and shipping something I actually use.

## Tech stack

- **React** (single-file, no build step)
- **Claude API** (Anthropic) for AI features
- **localStorage** for persistence

## How to run it

1. Clone this repo
2. Open `foodie-app.jsx` in any React environment, or drop it into a tool like [StackBlitz](https://stackblitz.com) or [CodeSandbox](https://codesandbox.io)
3. Add your Anthropic API key where prompted
4. Start planning
